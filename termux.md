curl -fLo ~/.vim/autoload/plug.vim --create-dirs \ https://raw.githubusercontent.com/junegunn/vim-plug/master/plug.vim

For vim it will be installed in ~/.vim/autoload and for neovim it will be in ~/.local/share/nvim/site/autoload ("${XDG_DATA_HOME:-$HOME/.local/share}"/nvim/site/autoload/plug.vim)
